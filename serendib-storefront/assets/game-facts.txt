SERENDIB STILL BREATHES
A game by Studio 141

Survive a post-apocalyptic Colombo in a single-player, third-person action adventure.
Explore a journey from an isolated bunker through Jungle Gorge and into the streets of Colombo. Encounter zombies and hostile survivors in a Sri Lankan setting.
