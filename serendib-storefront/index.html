ABOUT THIS GAME

PARADISE DIDN’T DISAPPEAR.IT CHANGED.

Beyond the silence, Sri Lanka still breathes. Nature pushes through concrete. Familiar streets become unfamiliar territory. And every path forward asks something of you.

Serendib Still Breathes is a single-player, third-person action adventure set in post-apocalyptic Colombo. Begin in an isolated bunker, cross the rocky paths of Jungle Gorge, and reach a city changed by survival.

AN ISLAND RECLAIMED

Explore a Sri Lankan setting, from dense jungle and moss-covered stone to weathered city boulevards beneath the Lotus Tower.

FIND YOUR WAY FORWARD

Climb, traverse, and look for a route through the wilderness. Push into the streets of Colombo, where zombies and hostile survivors stand between you and the way ahead.

  • A single-player journey across three distinct environments.
  • Third-person exploration and traversal.
  • Encounters with zombies and hostile survivors.
  • A post-apocalyptic world grounded in Sri Lanka.
