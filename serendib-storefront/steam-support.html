When will Serendib Still Breathes release?

The game is in development. A release date, price, and public game download have not been announced.

What are the system requirements?

The current development build is for Windows PC. Minimum and recommended specifications will be confirmed after representative hardware testing. No unverified hardware requirements are listed on this preview.

Can I connect my Steam account or buy the game here?

This is an independent Steam-style presentation mockup. It is not affiliated with Valve or connected to Steam. There are no login, payment, or game-purchase flows. Wishlist and Follow actions are local demonstrations.

Where are the trailer and press kit?

The trailer is available on the store preview and the game website. Download the 30-second trailer, press kit, or game fact sheet directly.

What kind of gameplay can I expect?

A single-player, third-person action adventure with exploration, traversal, zombies, and hostile survivors in a post-apocalyptic Sri Lankan setting. The project includes a bunker, Jungle Gorge, and Colombo.

How do the demo wishlist and discussions work?

The preview saves wishlist, follow, discussion, and reply state in this browser using local storage. These actions do not affect any real Steam account. Your browser may clear this data when you clear site storage.

How can I report an issue?

You can use the report builder below to create and download a text report. No report is sent automatically. A public player-support address has not been announced.
